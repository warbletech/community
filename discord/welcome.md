# 🐦 Welcome to **Warble Cloud**

You just joined the flock. Glad you're here.

Warble Cloud is a small team building **open, GCP-native AI ops** — five products, one philosophy: birds of a feather. This server is where we hang out, demo what we ship, and answer real questions from real operators.

## 🚀 Start here

**1. Read `#rules`.** It's two minutes. We take the Code of Conduct seriously: https://github.com/warble-tech/community/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md

**2. Pick your roles** in `#roles`. React to the emoji for areas you care about — Starling, Reflexion, Murmuration, Brain, FRAKMA, FinOps. We use these to ping you only when relevant.

**3. Say hi in `#introduce-yourself`.** Three lines is enough: who you are, what you run in production, and what brought you here.

## 🎙️ Office Hours — every Saturday, 7 PM IST

Open mic. Real Q&A. Live demos. Stack reviews if you ask in advance.

📅 Subscribe: https://warblecloud.com/community/office-hours/warble-office-hours.ics
🔉 Voice room: **Office Hours** under the **Help & Office Hours** category

## 🧭 Where to ask what

- `#help-and-questions` — anything technical, low ceremony, no question too small
- `#starling`, `#reflexion`, `#murmuration`, `#brain` — product-specific deep dives
- `#showcase` — share what you built. Customer stories, blog posts, demos welcome
- `#announcements` — read-only, for the things you'd be sad to miss

## 📦 The flock
