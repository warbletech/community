# Warble Cloud — server rules

We follow the [Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct](https://github.com/warble-tech/community/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Read it. The TL;DR is below.

## 1. Be excellent

Empathy first. Disagree on the substance, never on the person. Public criticism of code is fine — public criticism of people is not.

## 2. Stay on topic

Each channel has a purpose (described in its channel topic). Off-topic in `#off-topic` only.

## 3. No spam, no drive-bys

No referral links. No "join my Discord" cross-promotion. No DMing members you've never spoken to.

## 4. No NDA leaks

If your employer hasn't said it publicly, don't say it here. We'd rather you stay employed.

## 5. AI-generated answers must be marked

If you use an LLM to draft a reply in `#help`, label it `[LLM-drafted]` so others know to verify. We're not anti-AI — we're anti-hallucination-without-disclosure.

## 6. No `@everyone` / `@here` unless you're a Steward

Locked at the role level. Asking is fine — pinging without warning is not.

## 7. Office Hours are recorded

By joining the **Office Hours** voice room, you consent to recording. Mute / leave if you don't want to be recorded.

## 8. Reporting

DM any moderator, or email **conduct@warblecloud.com**. We respond within 48 hours and protect reporter privacy.

---

By staying in the server, you agree to these rules. Violations escalate per the [enforcement guidelines](https://github.com/warble-tech/community/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md#enforcement-guidelines): Correction → Warning → Temp ban → Permanent ban.
