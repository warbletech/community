# Office Hours

**Every Saturday · 7:00–8:00 PM IST · Discord voice room**

📅 **[Subscribe to the calendar (.ics)](https://warblecloud.com/community/office-hours/warble-office-hours.ics)**
🎙️ **[Join Discord](https://discord.gg/kAfR57mVDX)**
🐦 **[Schedule on warblecloud.com](https://warblecloud.com/community/office-hours)**

## Format

- 5 min — Welcome, intro, who's new
- 15 min — Demo of the week (rotating: Starling, Reflexion, Murmuration, Brain, FRAKMA)
- 25 min — Open Q&A / stack reviews / "ask me anything"
- 10 min — What's shipping next + community shoutouts
- 5 min — Wrap, links, what to bring next week

## Who facilitates

Rotating among Stewards and Working Group leads. Schedule lives in [`schedule.md`](schedule.md).

## Notes & recordings

Each session lands as a markdown file: `YYYY-MM-DD.md`. Template in [`TEMPLATE.md`](TEMPLATE.md).

After each session:

1. Open a PR with the session notes
2. Upload the recording to YouTube and link it in the notes
3. Cross-post highlights to [warblecloud.com/showcase](https://warblecloud.com/showcase) via the admin portal

## Anti-patterns

- ❌ Marketing pitches — this is a community space, not a sales channel
- ❌ Confidential roadmap leaks — public-only, in writing
- ❌ "Take it offline" — if a question matters to one person, it usually matters to ten
